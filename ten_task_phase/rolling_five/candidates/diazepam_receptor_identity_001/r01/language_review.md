# Constructor language and paired-condition check

Both English variants ask the same audit question and contain identical source, single-compound boundary, standard-field scope, seven category definitions, overlapping engineering flag, composition-conflict rule, species rule, counting unit, unresolved rule and output format. CG alone adds three short operational steps. GO's abbreviation wording is an evidence requirement; it does not prescribe a publication retrieval step.

Scientific terms are explained by their role in the comparison: a defined complex identifies constituent subunit kinds, a group does not specify coexistence, homologous attribution does not become direct just because the description is detailed. Output is a short two-column audit rather than130raw activity rows or a padded five-column mixed table.

Predicate quotations and competing interpretation checks are in pair_alignment.json. This is the constructor's review, not independent content or human validation. Difficulty is untested.
